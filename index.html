What is a LitBuy spreadsheet?

A LitBuy spreadsheet is a product research list used to organize finds before buying through a shopping agent. Useful rows keep the source link, selected option, price, measurements and unanswered questions together. The format may be a website, Google Sheet or personal comparison table.

This page combines a CAFANS catalog selection with a buying checklist. These records are browsing references; inclusion does not confirm that a particular item is available through LitBuy. Open CAFANS, inspect the product details and confirm the supported agent and current purchase options before proceeding.

How to use this LitBuy spreadsheet guide

Find a candidate

Filter the imported selection by name, category or reference budget. Record the CAFANS product ID so you can identify the same listing later. An attractive image is a starting point for research.

Confirm the exact item

Open CAFANS and search for the product name or ID. Check its current price, selected size, color, quantity and source details. Confirm agent compatibility before transferring any listing into LitBuy.

Review before shipping

If you order through an agent, review your own order details and warehouse photos. Resolve missing pieces, incorrect options and measurement questions before approving international dispatch.

Make room for delivery

Plan the full cost.

Enter your own USD estimates. Include domestic delivery, service fees, payment charges and applicable taxes in “Other charges.” Unknown costs still need a quote.

Fit checks for clothing and shoes

Measure a garment you already wear and compare equivalent dimensions with the listing chart. Save chest width, shoulder width, body length or inseam as appropriate, together with the unit and measurement method.

For shoes, keep the stated sizing system and foot-length guidance together. Foot length and insole length are different measurements. Ask for clarification when a chart does not explain which one it uses.

A practical QC photo checklist

Compare the received item with the option you selected: color, size label, quantity and included pieces. Request close-ups or measurements when standard photos do not answer a specific question.

Inspect visible seams, fasteners, marks and damage. Photos can help identify discrepancies, but cannot establish authenticity, material composition or long-term durability. Raise concerns within the terms applicable to your order.

LitBuy spreadsheet FAQ

Is this the official LitBuy website?

No. This is an independent product research page using a sourced CAFANS catalog selection. It does not operate LitBuy accounts, accept payments or manage shipping.

Where do the product buttons take me?

The “LitBuy spreadsheet” and “Buy it now” buttons open https://cafans.com. Search CAFANS using the product name or the record ID displayed on each card to locate the relevant item.

Are these live prices or shipping-inclusive totals?

No. The displayed amounts are CAFANS estimated USD item prices captured on November 19, 2026. They are reference amounts, exclude unconfirmed charges and may vary by option or later price changes.

Can every CAFANS find be ordered through LitBuy?

Compatibility is not verified by this page. Check the source listing and the currently supported purchase options. Confirm with your chosen agent that it can handle the exact item before paying.

Are the product images QC photos?

No. They are source listing images from the CAFANS catalog. QC photos should relate to the actual item received for your own order and the specific option you purchased.

Does the catalog refresh automatically?

No. This page contains six imported records from a dated catalog snapshot. Search and budget filters work on those records only. Revisit CAFANS for current availability, prices and its wider catalog.

What should I save in my own spreadsheet?

Start with the product name, source URL, listing ID, selected option, quantity, original price, currency and checked date. Add measurements, delivery estimates, order-specific QC references and any questions that remain unresolved.
